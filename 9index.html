THE INCREDIBLES

i

Mr. Incredible

Bob Parr is a man of immense strength and a strong sense of justice. As a father, he leads his family through every battle with courage. He is physically nearly invulnerable and can lift massive objects with ease.

i

Elastigirl

Helen Parr is a master of flexibility. She can stretch any part of her body up to 30 meters and reshape herself into various forms like parachutes or boats. She is the tactical mind of the team.

i

Violet Parr

Violet has the power of invisibility and force-field generation. She is the team's defensive specialist, protecting her family from explosions and enemy attacks with her purple energy shields.

i

Dash Parr

Dash is a speedster with a competitive spirit. He can run fast enough to travel across water without sinking. His reflexes are incredibly sharp, making him a difficult target for any villain.
